🎮 About SnakeRush

SnakeRush is a simple, fast and mobile-friendly Snake Game where your goal is to collect food, grow your snake and achieve the highest score possible.

🎯 Game Objective

  • 🍎 Collect as much food as possible
  • 🐍 Grow your snake
  • 🏆 Beat your High Score
  • 💥 Avoid walls and your own body

📱 Controls

  • 📱 Mobile — Swipe anywhere on the game board
  • ⌨️ Desktop — Arrow Keys
  • 🎮 Desktop — W / A / S / D
  • ⏸️ Press P or Space to pause

⚡ Snake Speed Modes

🐢 SLOW Relaxed
🐍 MEDIUM Normal
⚡ FAST Challenge

🏆 Scoring

  • 🍎 Every food = +10 points
  • 🐍 Every food increases snake size
  • 🏆 Highest score is saved automatically

💥 Game Over

The game ends when the snake hits the wall or its own body. Your score and best score will then be displayed, with an option to play again.

✨ Features

  • 🐍 Classic Snake gameplay
  • 📱 Mobile swipe controls
  • ⚡ Three speed modes
  • 🏆 High Score
  • 📊 Statistics
  • ⏸️ Pause / Resume
  • 🔊 Sound effects
  • 📳 Vibration support

💾 Saved Settings

Your selected speed, sound preference, vibration preference and game statistics are saved in your browser automatically.
