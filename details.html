🎮 About SnakeRush

SnakeRush is a simple, fast and mobile-friendly Snake Game. Collect food, grow your snake and try to achieve the highest score possible.

🎯 Game Objective

  • 🍎 Collect food to earn points
  • 🐍 Grow your snake after collecting food
  • 🏆 Try to beat your High Score
  • 💥 Avoid the walls
  • 🚫 Don't hit your own snake body

📱 Controls

  • 📱 Mobile — Swipe anywhere on the game board
  • ⬆️ Swipe Up — Move Up
  • ⬇️ Swipe Down — Move Down
  • ⬅️ Swipe Left — Move Left
  • ➡️ Swipe Right — Move Right
  • ⌨️ Desktop — Arrow Keys
  • 🎮 Desktop — W / A / S / D
  • ⏸️ P / Space — Pause Game

⚡ Snake Speed Modes

Choose your preferred snake speed from the Settings menu.

🐢 SLOW Relaxed
🐍 MEDIUM Normal
⚡ FAST Challenge

🏆 Scoring

  • 🍎 Each food gives +10 points
  • 🐍 Each food increases snake size
  • 📈 Your score increases as you play
  • 🏆 The best score is saved automatically

💥 Game Over

The game ends when the snake hits the wall or its own body. After the game ends, your score and High Score are shown and you can start a new game.

✨ Features

  • 🐍 Snake Game
  • 📱 Mobile-friendly controls
  • 👆 Swipe gameplay
  • ⚡ Slow / Medium / Fast modes
  • 🏆 High Score
  • 📊 Statistics
  • ⏸️ Pause / Resume
  • 🔊 Sound effects
  • 📳 Vibration support

💾 Saved Settings

SnakeRush saves your selected speed, sound preference, vibration preference and game statistics in your browser. This allows your settings and scores to remain available when you return to the game.
